About this site

Ren Data tracks price, rent and gross rental yield for 597 Spanish cities and towns, using official public sources (INE, Spain's Ministry of Housing) rather than scraped listing prices. The full site is in Spanish; this English section covers the 20 cities most commonly searched by international buyers, with added context on the property-buying process and non-resident taxation that doesn't apply to Spanish-resident buyers.
